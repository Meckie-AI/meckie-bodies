Bodies.

Open-hardware vehicles a household Meckie can inhabit. Each one runs its reflexes and senses locally and streams to Central for thinking. Print it, flash it, and it is a body.

Verify before you print. No STL here has been sliced or test-printed. Each pack's README.txt lists the first test prints, and the servo spline position along the STS3215 / STS3032 case is not published — every servo pocket carries 3 mm of shim room, and the first build of each body should measure it and record what it finds.

Meckie bodies are open hardware (CERN-OHL-S-2.0) and open code (MIT). Meckie OS is free to download; the Meckie brain is a paid service. A body flashes and runs its reflexes without an account — it needs one to think and talk.
