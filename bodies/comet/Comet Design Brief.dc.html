Knee linkage
The knee servo sits directly above the hip-pitch servo inside the bracket sleeve. An 18 mm crank drives an M3 pushrod to a 23 mm horn on the shin, a 1.28:1 reduction, so the STS3215 sees 22% less torque than the knee joint. Crank and horn are clocked at 152° so the rod meets both at about 82° when standing; across the working range (thigh ±20°, knee ±30°) the transmission angle stays above 35°. The four-bar couples thigh and knee angles; inverse kinematics compensates.
Wiring & strain relief
All three servos sit at or above the hip. The only cable below the hip is a 6-core flat cable for the wheel motor and encoder, clipped along the thigh, with a loop at the knee and strain relief at the foot cup. Each leg's chain enters its pod through a grommet on the abduction axis with a 30 mm service loop, a P-clip on the bracket, and a second clip at the chassis exit.

Gait modes · skate-walk

The legs always step. Above walking pace, each stance foot's wheel rolls forward while it's on the ground, so every step covers more distance at the same cadence. Body speed = leg contribution (up to ≈ 0.3 m/s) + wheel contribution. A foot about to land spins its wheel up to ground speed first, so touchdown is scuff-free.

Walk0–0.3 m/s
Wheels held by the motors in position-hold, so it's a true walk. The default mode, and used on carpet, thresholds, near drop-offs, and whenever the front depth sensor sees clutter.
Skate-walk0.3–1.5 m/s
Trot cadence of about 1.4 Hz, with lower 15 mm foot lift. At 1.4 m/s, wheels supply about 80% of the speed. Wide arcs only: wheel skid plus abduction steering.
Glidepose
Legs still in a crouched "surfing" stance while he rolls. Used for short flourishes and greetings, not for getting around.
Control split
Pi: gait planner, mode blend, IK at 100 Hz, streamed to the ESP32. ESP32-S3: servo bus, wheel speed loops at 1 kHz, touchdown spin-up, drop-off and obstacle reflexes, heartbeat watchdog.

Wiring diagram

VBAT5 VServo busESP32 I/OPi I/OUART link

Per the shared core, the ESP32-S3 is the reflex brain: it owns the servo bus, wheels, every ranging sensor and the servo power. The Pi plans the gait and solves IK, then streams joint targets to the ESP32 at 100 Hz. The servo bus moved off the Pi so the ESP32 can still run the safety routine if the Pi stops.

Snap features are sized for PLA: 1.2 mm cantilever thickness, 0.6 mm hook depth, 30° lead-in, 45° release face so they can be unsnapped with a fingernail or spudger. 07

Safety reflex · settles into a low crouch

{{ s.t }}{{ s.h }}{{ s.d }}
Triggers
No Central heartbeat for 500 ms (relayed by the Pi) · no Pi link for 200 ms · pack below 15% or 10.5 V · any servo at 70 °C or above · eFuse fault.
Always-on local reflexes
Edge: any drop-off ToF sees the floor fall away → wheels brake and the gait stops; it steps back only on command. Obstacle: forward lidar under 150 mm → wheels brake, walk speed capped. Fall: tilt past 40° → all servos to 10% torque.
Rest pose
Low crouch: thighs 20° further back, knees folded to keep the feet under the hips, body about 50 mm lower. Holds at 25% servo torque. If power is cut it settles onto its belly.
Resume
Stands up only on a fresh command from Central. Restoring the heartbeat alone does not wake it.

Verify before CAD freeze
  • STS3215 spline position along the 45.2 mm side is not published; measure it and shim each servo so the spline lands on its axis before closing the sleeves.
  • Collision sweep of the printed parts covers abduction ±12°, thigh ±20° and knee ±30° from stance. Rear legs: thigh and knee bent the same way together beyond about 40° is unreachable for the rod; keep the gait inside that.
  • Set each pushrod to length (front 149.5 mm, rear 129.2 mm centre to centre) and trim knee zero with the turnbuckle.
  • Servo masses with horns removed; the 74.5 g / 55 g figures may already include the stock horn.
  • Four-bar transmission angle across the full knee range; keep it above 35° at deepest crouch.
  • Pod louver area against knee-servo case temperature after 10 minutes of continuous trotting.
  • Charger heat in the tail at 25 W: give the BQ25798 a copper pour to the rear bulkhead, and derate to 1.5 A if the board passes 70 °C.
  • USB-C PD compatibility across phone, laptop and GaN chargers, plus the 5 V fallback path.
  • STS3215 12 V datasheet figures (speed, stall, continuous rating) and thermal behaviour in the knee position during long skate-walks.
  • Mic noise floor while walking and skate-walking, and whether servo whine and speaker echo need a mechanical fix or software is enough.
  • Wheel touchdown matching: measure the speed mismatch at foot contact, aiming for under 5% at 1.4 m/s.
  • Front ToF cover window: use IR-transparent material with an air gap or gasket, and check for crosstalk against the camera lens ring.
  • Drop-off ToF aim against the front-foot swing envelope, so a lifted foot never reads as a cliff.
