SCOUT. print parts

Print-ready STLs in millimetres, Z up, each in its print orientation. Every threaded joint has a heat-set insert bore. Joints that don't screw have snap hooks and matching windows. No glue anywhere.
Test-fit before a full printPrint a lower-shell corner and one wheel hub first to check your printer's hole tolerance. Interfaces are set from datasheets: N20 gearmotor 12 × 10 gearbox, Ø3 D-shaft (2.5 flat), 10 mm shaft; GC9A01 1.28" round module Ø39 board; XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense 21 × 17.8; 12 mm steel caster ball; 1S LiPo 503450 (50 × 34 × 5, 1000 mAh) in a snap-in floor cradle (pouches up to 50 × 34.6 × 7 fit). Sensor boards and small breakouts are clamped, so any VL53L0X / VL53L1X carrier works. Inserts: M2 Ø3.2, M2.5 Ø3.6, M3 Ø4.0.
