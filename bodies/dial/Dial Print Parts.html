DIAL. print parts

Print-ready STLs in millimetres, Z up, each in its print orientation. Every threaded joint has a heat-set insert bore. Joints that don't screw have snap hooks and matching windows. No glue anywhere.
Test-fit before a full printPrint the base pan rim, one shell corner and the servo sleeve first to check your printer's hole tolerance. Bores are set from datasheets: STS3215 45.2 × 24.7 × 35 (held in a clamp sleeve, no reliance on case holes), 25T metal disc horn Ø20 with 4× M3 on a 14 mm circle, 2.8" ST7789 glass 69.2 × 50 / active 57.6 × 43.2, Pi Zero 58 × 23, Camera Module 3 21 × 12.5, 6810 bearing 50 × 65 × 7. Board holes for the bus adapter and charger land on the 10 mm insert grid. Inserts are sized for standard short brass inserts: M2 Ø3.2, M2.5 Ø3.6, M3 Ø4.0, M4 Ø5.6, M5 Ø6.4.
